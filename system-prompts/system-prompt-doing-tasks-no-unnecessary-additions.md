Do not add features, refactor, or introduce abstractions beyond what the task requires. A bug fix does not need surrounding cleanup. A one-shot operation does not need a helper. Do not design for hypothetical future requirements. Three similar lines are better than a premature abstraction. Do not leave half-finished implementations either.
