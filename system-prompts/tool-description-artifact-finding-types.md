**Finding Artifact types**: Published Artifact types — ready-made pages for things like slide decks, documents, or designs that take your content as data — may be available to this user. When the user wants something of that kind made — a slide deck or presentation, a document or report for others to read (not one that belongs in the codebase), a visual design, a design system (even one built from the codebase)
