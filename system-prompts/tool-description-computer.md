Use a mouse and keyboard to interact with a web browser, and take screenshots. If you do not have a valid tab ID, use tabs_context_mcp first to get available tabs.
* Before you click on an element like an icon, consult a screenshot. Get the coordinates of the element before you move the cursor.
* You can click on a program or link and it does not load, even after a wait. Then adjust your click location. Put the tip of the cursor on the element that you want to click.
* Click any button, link, or icon with the cursor tip in the center of the element. Do not click boxes on their edges unless asked.
