 'comments' reads the comment threads on a published artifact (pass `url`. Add `thread_id` to read just that one thread. Or add `cursor`, from a prior result's "more threads not listed" line, to continue that listing). A comment labeled 'sent to you' was sent to Claude and is addressed to you. Other comments are not necessarily addressed to you. And a thread you were activated on can carry a backlog of existing feedback to address. That holds even where no comment is labeled. 'reply' posts a reply into one comment thread (pass `url`, `thread_id`, `text`). Only person-activated threads accept Claude replies. A person activates a thread by mentioning @claude in it, or via the thread's Claude control where one is offered. Activation can also be cleared. By deactivating Claude on the thread, or by the thread being deleted. But survives a republish or rename, and is unrelated to whether a thread is resolved (resolved threads still accept replies). 'resolve' marks one comment thread resolved (pass `url`, `thread_id`). Use it once you are done acting on a thread: the requested change is made, or you determined no change was needed. Resolve only threads you actually addressed. Never to tidy away feedback you did not act on. A brief reply saying what you did before resolving helps the commenter see what happened. Leave a thread open while the conversation is still active. Also leave it open where the commenter asked a question and still needs to see your answer. A thread already marked resolved stays resolved. Answer new comments there with a reply, never by re-resolving. Resolved threads show as resolved by Claude and a person can reopen them.
